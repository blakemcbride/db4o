\section*{dRS}

\href{http://www.db4o.com/}{Versant Inc.} supplies the db4o Replication System (dRS) under the General Public License (GPL). Under the GPL dRS is free to be used:

\begin{itemize}
\item for development,
\item in-house as long as no deployment to third parties takes place,
\item together with works that are placed under the GPL themselves.
\end{itemize}

You should have received a copy of the GPL in the file dRS.license.html together with the dRS distribution.

\section*{Bundled 3rd Party Licenses}

The dRS distribution comes with several 3rd party libraries, located in the /lib/ subdirectory together with the respective license files.
